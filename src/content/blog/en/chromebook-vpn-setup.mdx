Chromebook VPN setup differs from Windows or macOS. Three main paths: Play Store app, built-in VPN profile, and [router VPN](/blog/router-vpn-setup-guide).

## Play Store App (Recommended)

On Android-enabled Chromebooks:

1. Open Google Play Store
2. Install your VPN provider's app
3. Sign in, pick server, connect
4. Enable [kill switch](/blog/vpn-kill-switch-explained) and auto-connect

Older Chromebooks without Play Store need other methods.

## Built-in VPN Profile

Settings → Network → VPN → Add:

- **L2TP/IPsec** — enterprise or legacy providers
- **OpenVPN** — if provider supplies `.ovpn` (Chrome OS version dependent)

School-managed devices may enforce policy VPN.

## Router or Hotspot

Without Play Store:

- Home [router VPN](/blog/router-vpn-setup-guide)
- Phone VPN + hotspot to Chromebook (temporary)

## Streaming and School Networks

School networks may block VPN. Try obfuscated servers — [high-restriction guide](/blog/china-russia-ai-access-vpn). For streaming see [VPN streaming guide](/blog/vpn-streaming-and-content-access) and [best VPN](/en-iyi).
