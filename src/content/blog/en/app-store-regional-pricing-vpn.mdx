App Store and Google Play prices follow account region — IP alone may not be enough. VPN still helps research and new-account flows; see [PlayStation/Xbox store](/blog/playstation-xbox-store-vpn) and [software subscriptions](/blog/software-subscription-vpn-savings).

## Regional Pricing Logic

- Apple/Google account country sets currency and price
- Payment method — local card or gift card may be required
- IP signal matters when creating new accounts
- Some apps are region-locked

## VPN Use Cases

1. Price comparison across countries
2. New account setup — VPN + target country (ToS risk)
3. Regional in-app catalogs

- Subscription support issues on wrong region
