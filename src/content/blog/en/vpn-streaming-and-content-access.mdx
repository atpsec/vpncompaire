Streaming libraries vary by country because of licensing agreements, and catalog sizes change over time. A VPN can change the broad IP-location signal seen by a platform, but a different catalog is not guaranteed because account, payment, device-location, and VPN-detection checks may also apply.

## What is Geographic Restriction?

Geographic restriction (geo-blocking) is when content providers limit their services to specific countries. There are several reasons:

### Licensing Agreements

Movie and TV show producers license their content regionally. For example, a show's US broadcast rights might be sold to Netflix, UK rights to Amazon Prime, and other regions to different platforms.

This results in the same content being on different platforms in different countries or not being available at all.

### Legal Regulations

Some countries impose censorship or restrictions on content. Platforms don't show certain content in some regions to avoid legal issues.

### Pricing Strategies

Platforms apply different prices based on purchasing power. They offer cheaper subscriptions in developing countries and want to prevent these subscriptions from being used in other countries.

## How Does a VPN Affect Geographic Restrictions?

A VPN replaces the public IP address seen by a site with the selected server's address. That is only one of the location signals a platform may use.

**The process works like this:**

1. Open the VPN app
2. Connect to a server in your desired country (e.g., US)
3. Open the streaming platform
4. The platform sees the VPN server's IP, but may also evaluate account, payment, device-location, and known-VPN-IP signals
5. If the platform permits it, the regional catalog may change; access varies by server, account, and current platform controls

**Japan**: The anime catalog may differ from other regions

Changing server country may show a different catalog, but catalog counts and access change too often to promise a fixed increase. Check the current result in your account; see our [Netflix regional libraries with VPN](/blog/netflix-regional-libraries-vpn) guide for methods and limitations.

### Netflix VPN Blocking

Netflix has been actively trying to block VPN usage since 2016. If you get a proxy error, Netflix has detected your VPN:

```
"You seem to be using a proxy or unblocker"
```

**How Netflix Detects VPNs:**

1. **IP Databases**: Lists known VPN server IPs
2. **Many Users from Same IP**: Suspects if thousands of users connect from the same IP
3. **DNS Leak Detection**: If your DNS queries show a different country
4. **WebRTC Leak**: If your real IP leaks

### VPNs That Publish Netflix Support

Some providers publish Netflix support or troubleshooting documentation. These are provider statements, not VPN Advisor lab results or guarantees of uninterrupted access:

- **ExpressVPN**: Publishes Netflix support and troubleshooting documentation

Results can vary by country, server, device, and account, and platforms may block VPN IPs at any time. Check current provider documentation and verify with your own account.

## Best Practices for VPN Streaming

### 1. Choose the Right Server

- **For speed**: A physically close, lightly loaded server is often a good starting point
- **For content**: Country where desired library is located
- **For congestion**: A lower-load server may perform better

### 2. Use Wired Connection

Ethernet can be more stable than congested or interference-prone Wi-Fi. A VPN can add routing and encryption overhead; the actual effect depends on your network, protocol, and server load.

### 3. Protocol Selection

- **WireGuard**: Often efficient on modern devices; compare it on your own network
- **OpenVPN**: Mature and widely supported; performance varies by configuration
- **IKEv2**: Can reconnect quickly during mobile network changes

### 4. Use Split Tunneling

Routing only the streaming app through the VPN can reduce unrelated traffic on the VPN server. The stream's own speed still depends on the VPN server and network conditions.

### 5. App Instead of Browser

Apps and browsers can expose different location signals. Which works varies by platform and device; try both only in line with the service terms.

### 6. Clear Cookies and Cache

A streaming platform may remember a previous location in cookies or app data. Incognito mode or clearing cookies can remove stale data, but it does not guarantee that a VPN block will be removed.

## Legal Status

Is using VPN to bypass geographic restrictions legal?

The legal, contractual, and copyright consequences of VPN use and geographic circumvention vary by country and service. Paying for a subscription does not necessarily grant rights to every region's catalog. A platform may restrict playback, request verification, or take account action under its terms. Check the current service terms and the law where you are; this section is not legal advice.

## Streaming VPN Issues and Solutions

### Issue: Getting "Proxy Error"

**Solutions:**
- Connect to a different VPN server
- Turn VPN off and on
- Clear browser cookies
- Try a different VPN protocol

### Issue: Slow Loading or Buffering

**Solutions:**
- Switch to closer server
- Use WireGuard protocol
- Enable split tunneling
- Watch during less busy hours

### Issue: Low Video Quality

**Solutions:**
- Connect to faster server
- Temporarily turn off VPN and manually increase video quality, then turn VPN back on
- Use wired connection

## Conclusion

A VPN can change the IP-location signal seen by a platform and may show a different catalog in some cases. The result depends on the platform, account, device, server IP, and licensing terms.

However, remember:
- Price or brand alone does not establish platform compatibility
- Not all VPNs work with all platforms
- You may experience speed loss
- Platform terms and local law should be checked separately

Before choosing a provider, read its current support documentation and verify access and video quality with your own account, device, and network. No VPN can guarantee access to every title from every location.
