There are hundreds of VPNs on the market. All claim to be "the best", "fastest", "most secure". So how do you really choose the right VPN? Here are 12 critical factors you must check when choosing a VPN.

## 1. No-Logs Policy

If the purpose of using VPN is to protect your privacy, the VPN provider itself should not keep logs.

### What Does No-Logs Mean?

VPN provider should not record:
- Which websites you visit
- When you connect
- How much data you use
- Your real IP address
- DNS queries

### Acceptable Logs

Some minimal logs are technically necessary:
- Total bandwidth usage (not individual)
- Server load (anonymous)
- Connection count (without identity)

### How to Verify No-Logs?

**1. Read Privacy Policy**

Should be detailed, clear, and understandable. Avoid vague statements:
- ❌ "We store necessary information"
- ✅ "We keep no activity logs"

**2. Check Independent Audits**

Reliable audit firms:
- Deloitte
- PwC
- KPMG
- Cure53
- Securitum
- Assured AB

**Example**: ExpressVPN was audited by PwC and no-logs claim was verified.

**3. Public Legal Records**

Past cases and reported server seizures can show how a provider responded at a particular time. They are not standalone proof of the current policy or a future guarantee; verify the underlying decision or report directly.

### Red Flags

- No or vague privacy policy
- "We share data when legally required" statement
- Working with third-party ad networks
- Free VPN (may be making money by selling data)

## 2. Jurisdiction

Which country the VPN company is registered in directly affects your privacy.

### 5/9/14 Eyes Alliances

Intelligence sharing agreements:

**5 Eyes (Worst):**
- US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand
- Comprehensive surveillance, data sharing

**9 Eyes:**
- 5 Eyes + Denmark, France, Netherlands, Norway

**14 Eyes:**
- 9 Eyes + Germany, Belgium, Italy, Spain, Sweden

### Privacy-Friendly Countries

**Switzerland:**
- Strong privacy laws
- Outside 5/9/14 Eyes
- ProtonVPN, VyprVPN

**Panama:**
- No data retention laws
- NordVPN

**British Virgin Islands:**
- Not subject to UK laws
- ExpressVPN

**Romania:**
- EU member but flexible privacy laws
- CyberGhost

## 3. Encryption Standard

Strong encryption protects your data.

### Minimum Standard: AES-256

- **AES-256**: Military-grade encryption, impossible to break
- **AES-128**: Sufficient but AES-256 preferred
- **Blowfish**: Old, avoid
- **PPTP**: Broken, never use

### Encryption Protocols

- **ChaCha20**: Modern, fast (in WireGuard)
- **RSA-2048/4096**: For key exchange
- **SHA-256/SHA-512**: Hash function

### Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS)

Creates new encryption key for each session. If one key is stolen, only that session is affected.

**Check**: Ensure VPN supports PFS.

## 4. Kill Switch

Feature that completely cuts internet access when VPN connection drops.

### Why Critical?

VPN connection can sometimes drop:
- Server maintenance
- Network change
- Software bug

Without kill switch, your real IP and traffic are exposed at that moment.

### Kill Switch Types

**1. Application Level:**
- Inside VPN app
- Only active when VPN running

**2. System Level:**
- At operating system level
- Works even if VPN app closes

**Advice**: Prefer system-level kill switch. For a deeper look at how it works and what it actually blocks, see our [VPN kill switch explained](/blog/vpn-kill-switch-explained) guide.

### Testing

1. Turn on VPN
2. Manually disconnect
3. Internet access should be cut
4. Your IP should not leak

## 5. DNS Leak Protection

DNS leak is when your real DNS queries go to your ISP even while using VPN.

### What is DNS Leak?

Normal: VPN DNS → Encrypted
Leak: ISP DNS → Open

Your ISP sees which sites you visit.

### Testing

1. Turn on VPN
2. Go to dnsleaktest.com
3. Run "Extended Test"
4. Results should show VPN provider's DNS

**Red Flag**: If your ISP's DNS appears, there's a leak.

### Solution

Enable "Use VPN DNS" option in VPN settings.

## 6. Server Network

Wide server network provides better speed and access.

### Server Count

- **1000+ servers**: Good
- **3000+ servers**: Very good
- **5000+ servers**: Excellent

**Important**: Quality matters more than quantity. 10,000 slow servers are worse than 1,000 fast servers.

### Country Diversity

- **50+ countries**: Sufficient
- **80+ countries**: Good
- **100+ countries**: Excellent

**Countries You Need:**
- Home country (when abroad)
- US (Netflix, Hulu)
- UK (BBC iPlayer)
- Germany, Netherlands (European content)

### Physical vs Virtual Servers

**Physical Server**: Real server in that country
**Virtual Server**: Server in another country, IP from that country

**Example**: Physical server in some countries may be illegal, virtual server used.

**Issue**: Virtual servers sometimes slower.

## 7. Speed and Performance

VPN should be fast, otherwise unusable.

### Speed Factors

1. **Protocol and implementation**: Results depend on the provider's implementation as well as the protocol
2. **Server capacity and load**: A capacity label alone does not guarantee user performance
3. **User congestion**: Results can change with load
4. **Route and physical distance**: A nearby location often helps, but network routing is decisive

### Speed Test

Before buying:
- Read the refund conditions before paying
- Run speed tests on different servers
- If it does not fit, request a refund within the stated window and exclusions

**Expectation**: There is no universal speed-loss percentage. Compare VPN-on and VPN-off results on the same device, network and time window.

## 8. Device Support and Simultaneous Connections

VPN should work on all your devices.

### Platform Support

Must-have:
- Windows
- macOS
- iOS
- Android
- Linux

Bonus:
- Router
- Smart TV
- Fire TV Stick
- Browser extension

### Simultaneous Connections

- **5 devices**: Standard
- **6-10 devices**: Good
- **Unlimited**: Excellent (Surfshark)

**Calculation**: Phone + Laptop + Tablet + Smart TV + Family members = 5+ devices

## 9. Streaming Support

Access to platforms like Netflix, Disney+, BBC iPlayer.

### Streaming Test

Ensure VPN supports:
- Netflix US, UK, Japan
- Disney+
- Amazon Prime Video
- BBC iPlayer
- Hulu

**Check**: Look at "streaming" section on VPN's website.

### Special Streaming Servers

Some VPNs offer streaming-optimized servers:
- NordVPN: SmartPlay
- CyberGhost: Streaming servers
- Surfshark: All servers streaming-supported

## 10. Torrent and P2P Support

If you use torrent, VPN should support P2P.

### P2P Policy

- **P2P on all servers**: Best (Mullvad, Surfshark)
- **Special P2P servers**: Good (NordVPN, CyberGhost)
- **P2P forbidden**: Avoid

### Port Forwarding

Increases torrent speed. Some VPNs support:
- PIA
- AirVPN
- Mullvad

## 11. Customer Support

You should be able to get help when you have issues.

### Support Channels

**Must-have:**
- 24/7 live chat
- Email support

**Bonus:**
- Phone support
- Language support
- Detailed guides
- Video tutorials

### Response Time

- **Live chat**: 1-5 minutes
- **Email**: Within 24 hours

**Test**: Ask question in live chat before buying, evaluate response quality.

## 12. Price and Money-Back Guarantee

VPN should fit your budget.

### Price Ranges

**Budget Friendly**: $2-4/month
- Surfshark
- PIA

**Mid-Range**: $4-7/month
- NordVPN
- CyberGhost

**Premium**: $7-12/month
- ExpressVPN

**Fixed Price**: 5 euro/month
- Mullvad

### Money-Back Guarantee

**Standard**: 30 days
**Good**: 45 days (CyberGhost)

**Important**: Check if they actually refund (online reviews).
