YouTube Premium subscriptions are priced dramatically differently across countries for the exact same content. 13.99 USD/month in the US, around 2 USD in Turkey, ~1.8 USD in India, sometimes under 1 USD in Argentina depending on the peso. The Family plan ranges from 22.99 USD (US) to ~3.6 USD (India). All for the same ad-free videos, YouTube Music subscription, and background play. Buying via VPN from a low-price region can save 100-150 USD per year, but Google's controls require following certain rules to keep the subscription active.

This guide covers YouTube Premium regional pricing, the VPN purchase flow, family plan sharing, and account suspension risks. For streaming-related VPN choice, our [Netflix regional libraries article](/en/blog/netflix-regional-libraries-vpn) is a useful complement.

## How Big Are the Price Differences?

Approximate USD-equivalent monthly pricing:

- **US**: 13.99 USD individual, 22.99 USD Family (6 people)
- **UK**: 12.99 GBP (~16 USD)
- **Germany**: 12.99 EUR (~14 USD)
- **Turkey**: ~2 USD
- **India**: 149 INR (~1.8 USD), Family 299 INR (~3.6 USD)
- **Argentina**: ARS-based; with depreciation 1-3 USD range
- **Egypt**: 99 EGP (~2 USD)
- **Philippines**: 159 PHP (~2.8 USD)

In short: subscriptions at 15-20 percent of the US price are achievable. With 6-person family sharing, per-person annual cost can drop to 4-6 USD.

## Which Region Is Best?

Pick not just the cheapest, but the most stable:

**Turkey**: If you live in Turkey, no VPN needed — sign up directly. But the TRY price has been raised aggressively in recent renewals; price-tracking is essential.

**India**: Sterilized pricing is relatively stable. An Indian card or INR-capable virtual card is needed. UPI or Razorpay-based payment is common.

**Argentina**: Lowest USD equivalent; but peso depreciation triggers automatic price hikes. For non-resident users, payment is hard — local cards required.

**Egypt, Philippines**: Low prices and relatively easy virtual card acceptance.

## VPN Purchase Steps

The YouTube Premium signup flow has several regional details:

### 1. New Google Account (Recommended)

Your existing Google account may have its "payment region" already set to a specific country. A new account is a clean start. Google permits multiple accounts per person.

### 2. VPN in Target Region

Indian server (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore) for Indian pricing. WireGuard-based protocols are preferable for low latency.

### 3. Cookie and Cache Cleanup

The browser or YouTube app may remember the previous region's cookies. Start clean.

### 4. Region Verification

In Google Account > Payment methods, the country should show India. If it does not match, the Premium signup will not work, or it will register at the wrong price.

### 5. Payment Method

A card supporting INR is required. Foreign cards are declined ~95 percent of the time. Workarounds: Wise virtual card, Revolut, or help from a contact based in India.

### 6. After Signup

You can disable the VPN and use YouTube from your normal connection. The Premium subscription remains active. Only **renewals** and **billing** need the VPN switched back to the target region.

## Family Plan Strategy

YouTube Premium Family Plan supports up to 6 users. India's Family is 299 INR/month (~3.6 USD); split 6 ways, that's around 60 cents per person per month.

**Rules**:

- All members must share the family manager's home address (Google's official policy)
- Google occasionally enforces this via IP/location checks
- If users are clearly outside the family's address, the Family can be canceled

**Practice**: Most users distribute the family across cities; Google's enforcement is not strict, but obviously cross-country members can become a problem. The safest scenario is a family/friends group living in the same country.

## Account Suspension Risks

Google's enforcement on YouTube Premium is different from other services:

- **Low: One-off signup**: Subscribing from India via VPN, then watching from your home country and using VPN at renewal. Google rarely flags this.
- **Medium: Constant region switching**: Paying from a different country every month. Google may flag "suspicious activity."
- **High: Reselling family slots**: Selling Family Plan slots to strangers or via forums. If detected, the plan can be terminated.

Google tends to "adjust the price" rather than suspend accounts — meaning at the next renewal, the price reverts to the local rate. This is gentler than Netflix or ChatGPT account-suspension reflexes.

## Legal and Ethical Side

YouTube's Terms of Service require "payment method country compatible with residence region." PPP discounts are designed for local users; using a VPN from a developed country to capture them goes against Google's intent.

Irony for Turkey-based users: Turkey is already in the lowest pricing tier worldwide. Switching to India via VPN saves 20-30 USD/year; Argentina 30-40 USD. The complexity-savings tradeoff is debatable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**If Turkey is already cheap, do I need a VPN?**
Turkey is among the cheapest worldwide, but vulnerable to renewal hikes. India and Argentina offer more stable low prices. Compare and decide.

**Can family members join from different countries on a VPN-bought plan?**
Google's official policy is "same household," but enforcement is not user-IP-based. In practice cross-country members usually work fine, but the risk exists.

**Can I buy YouTube Premium from a mobile app via VPN?**
App Store/Google Play purchases are bound to the App Store country. You'd have to change the App Store country too — much more complex. Web purchase via VPN is the practical route.

**Do I need to cancel and re-buy when changing region?**
No. A one-time region setting is enough. With consistent payment method and account region, automatic renewal preserves the region. Only at renewal time does the VPN need to be in that region.
