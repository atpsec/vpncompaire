Global retailers sometimes list the same SKU at different prices per country — FX, tax, logistics and market strategy. VPN changes your IP; see also [Steam](/blog/steam-regional-pricing-vpn) and [software subscriptions](/blog/software-subscription-vpn-savings).

## How Regional Pricing Works

- Geo-targeting by IP or account country
- Currency conversion fees
- Shipping region — cheap price may not ship to you
- Logged-in account can override VPN region

## Safe Comparison with VPN

1. Private mode + clear cookies
2. VPN to target country
3. Same SKU and quantity
4. Real shipping address — don't fake country
5. [DNS leak test](/blog/dns-leak-test-vpn)

## Risks

- Payment decline if card country mismatches VPN
- Account restrictions under platform terms
- Phishing fake discount sites
- Privacy — purchase data still goes to the merchant
