Apple devices include several privacy features, and iCloud Private Relay is one of the most visible. It should not be treated as a full VPN replacement.

## Private Relay scope

Private Relay is designed to protect supported Safari web browsing and related traffic. It separates who can see the user's IP address from who can see the destination request.

## VPN scope

A VPN can protect traffic from many apps across the device, depending on the client and operating-system configuration. Corporate VPNs can also provide access to private resources.

## When they interact

Network policies, VPN profiles and enterprise management can affect whether Private Relay is available. If connectivity is inconsistent, test one privacy layer at a time to identify the routing path.

## 2026 checklist

- Verify which apps need VPN coverage.
- Check whether a work profile manages the network.
- Test DNS and IPv6.
- Keep iOS/iPadOS updated.
- Use passkeys or MFA for Apple ID and important services.

## Bottom line

Private Relay is a useful Apple privacy feature for supported traffic. A VPN provides broader routing control and remains relevant when you need protection across apps or access to private networks.
