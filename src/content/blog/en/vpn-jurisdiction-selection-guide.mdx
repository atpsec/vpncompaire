Where a VPN company is registered shapes how it can respond to data requests. "No-logs" is not independent of jurisdiction — courts and regulators matter.

## Why Jurisdiction Matters

Providers may:

- Face local court orders
- Participate in intelligence-sharing treaties
- Have assets frozen in that country

No logs reduces what can be handed over — but corporate structure, billing and support tickets still exist.

## 14 Eyes and Alliances

Intelligence-sharing groups ease cross-border data transfer. Privacy-focused users often prefer Switzerland, Iceland, Panama or BVI — context, not a guarantee.

| Example | Jurisdiction | Note |
|---------|--------------|------|
| Mullvad | Sweden | No-logs + anonymous payment focus |
| Proton VPN | Switzerland | Strong legal framework |
| NordVPN | Panama | Offshore structure |
| ExpressVPN | BVI | Offshore |
| Norton VPN | US | Consumer brand |

## No-Logs + Jurisdiction Together

Bad jurisdiction ≠ bad VPN automatically. Look for:

1. Independent [audit report](/blog/vpn-audit-report-guide)
2. Transparent incident reporting
3. RAM-only or minimal metadata policy
