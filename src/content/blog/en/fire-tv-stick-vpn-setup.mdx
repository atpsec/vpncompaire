Fire TV Stick has no system-wide VPN API — use an app, router, or Smart DNS. Same goals as [Apple TV](/blog/apple-tv-vpn-setup) and [Android TV](/blog/android-tv-vpn-setup) guides.

## Method 1: Fire TV VPN App

Many providers ship Fire OS apps:

1. **Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options** — allow unknown sources if needed
2. Search your VPN in Amazon Appstore
3. Install, sign in, pick server
4. Reopen Prime Video, Netflix, etc. after connecting

Some VPN apps may not appear in regional Appstores.

## Method 2: Router VPN

Fire Stick traffic exits through your home network. [Router VPN](/blog/router-vpn-setup-guide) protects the Stick without an app.

Pros: whole-home protection. Cons: location changes via router UI.

## Method 3: Smart DNS

For streaming geo-unblock only (MediaStreamer, SmartPlay, etc.):

- No VPN encryption — DNS routing only
- Fast streaming; weak for general privacy
- See [VPN streaming guide](/blog/vpn-streaming-and-content-access)

## Streaming Tips

- Clear app cache after VPN connect
- Some services block VPN IPs — [streaming VPN](/best-vpn/streaming) list
- [Prime Video regional catalog](/blog/amazon-prime-video-vpn-guide)

## Security

Run [DNS leak test](/blog/dns-leak-test-vpn). On untrusted networks consider router VPN or phone hotspot.

## Summary

Fire TV Stick VPN: Appstore app first; router for whole home; Smart DNS for streaming only. Use [methodology](/methodology) to compare documented platform support.
