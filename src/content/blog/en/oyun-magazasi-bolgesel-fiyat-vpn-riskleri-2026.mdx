Regional game pricing exists because publishers and platforms adapt prices to local markets. A VPN can change the visible IP location, but stores usually use more than IP address to determine account region.

## Account region matters

Platforms may consider billing address, payment method, previous purchases and explicit account-country settings. A VPN does not automatically change any of those.

## Policy risk

Attempting to obtain a price intended for another market may violate platform terms. Consequences can include failed purchases, region locks or account restrictions.

## Research versus purchase

Using a VPN to compare public list prices is different from misrepresenting account residency during checkout. For purchases, follow the platform's official region-change process.

## Cost comparison

Include taxes, exchange-rate fees, gift-card premiums and the ability to use DLC or subscriptions in your real region.

## Bottom line

Regional pricing can create large differences, but a VPN alone does not make an account eligible for another market. Protect the account first and follow platform rules when changing regions.
