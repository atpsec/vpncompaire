Software subscriptions are one of the fastest-growing parts of an annual budget. Adobe Creative Cloud All Apps is roughly 660 USD/year in the US; the same package can be cheaper in Turkey or India under regional pricing. Microsoft 365 Family, Notion Plus, Figma Professional, Canva Pro — each has noticeable regional spreads. Buying via VPN from a low-price region can save hundreds of dollars per year, but platforms add friction at two stages: identity checks at payment and region audits at renewal.

This guide covers regional pricing for Adobe, Microsoft, Notion, Figma, and Canva, the VPN purchase flow, the risks, and renewal strategy. Our [ChatGPT Plus savings article](/en/blog/chatgpt-access-turkey-vpn) applies the same logic to AI services; this article focuses on traditional software subscriptions.

## How Big Are the Regional Spreads?

A few critical examples:

**Adobe Creative Cloud All Apps**: 59.99 USD/month with annual commitment in the US. Around 4,230 INR/month (~50 USD) in India. 309 BRL/month (~60 USD) in Brazil. Regional spreads of 15-20 percent are normal.

**Microsoft 365 Family** (6 users, 1 TB OneDrive): 99.99 USD/year in the US. ~30 USD equivalent in Turkey. 6,199 INR/year (~75 USD) in India.

**Notion Plus**: 10 USD/month in the US, similar across most regions. PPP discounts are limited; education accounts are entirely free with an .edu address.

**Figma Professional**: 15 USD/month/seat, globally consistent. Regional spread is small; Education Plan is free.

**Canva Pro**: 14.99 USD/month in the US, with notably lower prices in India and parts of SE Asia.

**JetBrains All Products Pack**: 289 USD/year for individuals, globally consistent; no regional discount. Students free.

General rule: Adobe, Microsoft, and Canva show meaningful regional spreads; Notion, Figma, and JetBrains do not.

## Two Scenarios for Buying Cheaper

There are two distinct strategies:

### Scenario A: Local price already cheaper than US

If you live in a low-PPP region, you don't need a VPN — open a local account, pay with a local card. The savings are already in your favor.

**Risk**: Providers can re-index local prices and renewals can rise as exchange rates or regional policies change. Check the current renewal total rather than assuming the introductory gap will persist.

### Scenario B: Buying from another low-PPP region with VPN

If your local price is high, or you want a deeper discount, VPN-based purchase from a low-PPP region (India, Vietnam, Indonesia) is on the table. Adobe's India pricing is sometimes the cheapest globally.

**Risk**: A local card, local IP, and local billing address are typically required. Foreign cards paying in INR are often declined. Virtual card services (Wise, Revolut) can help but add a layer.

## VPN Purchase Steps

1. **Identify the target region**: Compare Adobe prices in 4-5 regions over VPN. Check India, Turkey, Indonesia, Brazil, and the Philippines.
2. **New account (recommended)**: An existing Adobe ID or Microsoft account may have its region locked. A new account picks up the regional price.
3. **VPN on + browser cleanup**: Clear cookies, open a fresh incognito window.
4. **Compatible payment method**: For an Indian IP, an Indian card or a virtual card supporting INR. PayPal region settings also matter. Foreign-card-foreign-currency sometimes works but is risky.
5. **Billing address**: A real address in the target region is needed. A "hotel address" or fake info violates the contract.
6. **Renewal strategy**: At renewal you must be in the same VPN region. If the card hasn't changed, an automatic renewal can flip the region.

## Account Suspension Risks

Adobe and Microsoft are stricter than most other digital products on subscription audits:

- **Adobe**: Region audit happens at renewal. If you switch to an Indian payment from a US account, expect a "price adjustment" email; the contract allows unilateral region changes.
- **Microsoft**: 365 subscriptions are tied to the Microsoft account country. Country change is allowed once per year; constant VPN switching can trigger audits.
- **Notion and Figma**: More flexible; the IP at payment sets the price, and post-purchase changes are rarely audited.
- **Canva**: Checks IP at renewal; if the region changes, the price adjusts.

## A Special Case: Adobe Education Plan

Adobe Creative Cloud's student/teacher plan is 19.99 USD/month with annual commitment in the US, all apps included. A valid .edu address or international student ID is required. This price is cheaper than any VPN PPP discount — a real alternative to VPN routes.

## Microsoft 365 and Family Sharing

Microsoft 365 Family allows sharing across 6 accounts. One person buys the plan and adds family/friends, dropping per-person cost below 5 USD. This stacks with regional pricing: buy the Family plan in a cheap region, share with 5-6 people. Managing a shared Microsoft account is operationally complex; recommended only for trusted groups.

## Mind the Renewal Trap

Many software subs work as "year-1 promo, year-2 list price." Adobe's annual commitment lifts to the standard price in year two. Two options:

1. **Cancel and reopen**: Adobe's "win-back" emails offer discounts; canceling and reopening 30 days later can recover a discount.
2. **New account + different region**: Open a fresh Adobe ID and buy from another PPP region. Files transfer through Creative Cloud.

## Ethical and Legal Side

Software subscription terms typically say "pay the price for your country of legal residence." PPP discounts are intended for local users. In practice:

- **Low risk**: Personal use, single annual subscription. Not in Adobe or Microsoft's audit focus.
- **Medium risk**: Constant region switching at renewal. Account suspension possible.
- **High risk**: Fake region on a corporate license. An audit can void the invoice and demand additional fees.

VPN use and regional-purchase rules vary by jurisdiction, while each software provider may impose separate account and payment terms. Check current local rules and official terms; this page is not legal advice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I buy Adobe Creative Cloud from India with a VPN?**
Theoretically yes; in practice payment is hard. You need an Indian card or an INR-capable virtual card. A foreign card can be tried but is usually declined.

**Can I change my Microsoft 365 region?**
Microsoft allows country change once per year. Changing the country ends the current subscription, and the new region's price applies on renewal. Plan ahead.

**Can I get Notion Plus cheaper through a VPN?**
Notion's PPP discounts are limited; most regions are similar. If you have an .edu email, the Education plan is free — better than any VPN discount.

**What if my Canva region changes at renewal?**
Canva checks IP at renewal; if the region differs, the price adjusts to the new region. Keep your VPN in the same region during renewal.

## Conclusion

Software subscriptions can run into thousands of dollars annually; regional spreads are not negligible. Adobe Creative Cloud, Microsoft 365, and Canva offer the most savings; for Notion, Figma, and JetBrains, education and open-source discounts are more practical than VPN tricks. If you go the VPN route, stay consistent in one region, ensure payment compatibility, and plan your renewal strategy.
