NordVPN and Surfshark are two of the most frequently confused premium VPNs — especially since 2022 when both came under the Nord Security umbrella. Many users ask: "Same company, which one should I pick?" The answer isn't simple because the products are still developed by separate teams and target different user profiles.

This comparison places them side-by-side across speed, infrastructure, pricing, security features, ease of use, and customer support. For other comparisons see our [ExpressVPN vs ProtonVPN article](/en/blog/expressvpn-vs-protonvpn-comparison) and the [VPN comparison page](/en/comparison) for general provider review.

## Quick Verdict

NordVPN: leads on raw speed, server diversity, advanced security features (Threat Protection, Meshnet). More expensive but fuller feature set.

Surfshark: leads on unlimited devices, lower price, ease of use. A few advanced features missing.

The clear answer below depends on your user profile.

## Server Count and Geography

**NordVPN**: 6,000+ servers across 60+ countries. From Turkey, near regions (Germany, Netherlands, Bulgaria) have heavy coverage. 1,900+ in US, 800+ in UK, 250+ in Germany.

**Surfshark**: 3,200+ servers across 100+ countries. Wider geographic spread but fewer per country. Performance from Turkey to common destinations close to NordVPN.

Practical impact: for high-volume use cases like US streaming or ChatGPT access, NordVPN's larger pool gives buffer against IP blocks. For reaching less-served countries (Bangladesh, Kenya), Surfshark covers more.

## Speed and network conditions

VPN Advisor does not publish a fixed laboratory speed result for this comparison. NordLynx and Surfshark WireGuard target low overhead; the real difference depends on route, server load, ISP, device and protocol settings. Before deciding, check ping, throughput and packet loss from your own connection using comparable servers.

For detailed protocol comparison see our [WireGuard vs OpenVPN article](/en/blog/wireguard-vs-openvpn-comparison).

**Surfshark**:

Surfshark's entry offer and bundle scope differ from NordVPN's; read plan contents and renewal prices together when comparing like for like.

## Device Count

NordVPN: 10 simultaneous devices.
Surfshark: unlimited.

Clear advantage to Surfshark for families or small teams. NordVPN's 10 devices are sufficient for an individual with 3-4 devices.

## Security Features

Both providers: AES-256 encryption, kill switch, no-logs (audited), DNS leak protection, IP leak protection.

**NordVPN extras:**

- Threat Protection: malware, tracker, ad blocker (system-wide)
- Meshnet: encrypted device-to-device network, P2P
- Onion over VPN: built-in Tor integration
- Double VPN: multi-country multi-hop
- Dedicated IP: optional add-on

**Surfshark extras:**

- Multihop: multi-country VPN chain (same function as NordVPN Double VPN)
- CleanWeb: ad and tracker blocker
- NoBorders: obfuscation for restricted countries
- Bypasser (split tunneling)
- Camouflage Mode: hide VPN traffic from ISPs
- Rotating IP: IP changes within minutes

Both have RAM-only server infrastructure. NordVPN has a richer audit history (PwC, Deloitte audit reports), Surfshark's first audits were completed from 2022 onward. Our [VPN privacy and security article](/en/blog/vpn-privacy-and-security) covers audits in depth.

## Kill Switch and Leak Protection

NordVPN: system-wide kill switch (Windows, Mac, Linux), app-level kill switch optional. Native on iOS and Android.

Surfshark: same coverage. No practical difference.

## Streaming Performance

Both providers publish streaming-related features and server purposes, but IP pools and platform policies change. Our [VPN streaming and content access article](/en/blog/vpn-streaming-and-content-access) provides a current self-check list.

## Ease of Use

Surfshark has a cleaner interface. The "fastest server" button is more prominent. Settings menus are flatter.

NordVPN exposes more features; this can feel busy to a new user. For power users the depth is the appeal.

iOS and Android apps look modern on both. Surfshark mobile is slightly more minimal.

## Customer Support

Both offer 24/7 live chat. Response times:

- NordVPN: 2-3 minute average to live agent
- Surfshark: 3-5 minute average

Knowledge base depth is slightly better at NordVPN. English-first on both; Turkish responses available but slower.

## Which Scenario Fits Which?

**Pick NordVPN if**:
- Speed is top priority
- You want Onion over VPN, Double VPN, advanced features
- Threat Protection and Meshnet matter
- 10 devices is enough
- Audit history is a key criterion

**Pick Surfshark if**:
- You need unlimited devices (family, small team)
- Budget is tighter
- You prefer a simpler UI
- Camouflage Mode or NoBorders obfuscation needed
- 100+ country coverage matters

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Same company — is data shared between them?**
No. Two products run on separate infrastructure with separate no-logs policies. Audits were independent.

**Which works more reliably from Turkey?**
Both providers document auto-reconnect and protocol options; stability depends on route, ISP and device conditions.

**Does multi-hop matter?**
For most users, no. For high-privacy needs NordVPN's Double VPN and Surfshark's MultiHop are similar; more in our [VPN for journalists and activists](/en/blog/vpn-for-journalists-activists) article.

**Refund policy?**
Check each provider's current refund terms; conditions can vary by campaign and purchase channel.

**Easier to cancel?**
Both via account dashboard. Surfshark's auto-renewal is slightly easier to disable; NordVPN may ask live-chat confirmation.

## Conclusion

NordVPN and Surfshark are different vaults of the same company. NordVPN is faster, more expensive, packed with advanced features. Surfshark is cheaper, simpler, with unlimited devices. Both clear the premium VPN quality bar.

For a clear decision compare device limits, documented features, price and renewal terms together; check performance on your own connection.
