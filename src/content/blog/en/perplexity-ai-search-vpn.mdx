Perplexity popularized AI search with cited sources — different from ChatGPT's general assistant flow. VPN users ask if search history and IP are hidden — partially yes, not full anonymity.

## How Perplexity Works

Your query hits their servers; the model fetches and summarizes web pages. That means:

- Query text and metadata may be logged
- IP and account on provider side
- Source links behave like normal web clicks

[AI tools privacy](/blog/ai-tools-privacy-vpn) explains VPN limits with AI metadata.

## What VPN Adds

- Encrypted link on public Wi-Fi
- Less ISP-level "you visited Perplexity" visibility (content still at Perplexity)
- Regional access or pricing scenarios — policies change

VPN does **not**:

- Stop server-side query processing
- Guarantee exclusion from training on free tiers — read policy
- Block [fingerprint](/blog/browser-fingerprint-vpn) tracking
