The price displayed on a subscription page may not be the amount that reaches your card statement. Currency conversion, tax and renewal rules can all change the total.

## IP location is only one signal

Some services localize prices using network location, but checkout may also validate account country, billing address and card origin. A VPN cannot change those attributes.

## Currency conversion

The final amount can depend on the merchant's currency, card-network conversion and the bank's foreign-transaction fee. Small regional price differences can disappear after conversion.

## Renewal matters

Introductory offers may renew at a standard rate. Record the next renewal amount and billing interval before subscribing.

## Comparison table

Track first-term price, renewal price, tax treatment, currency, bank fees and refund conditions. Compare the real total over the period you expect to keep the service.

## Bottom line

Use a VPN for market research, not as a substitute for legitimate billing information. Real savings are measured at checkout and renewal, not on the first localized landing page.
