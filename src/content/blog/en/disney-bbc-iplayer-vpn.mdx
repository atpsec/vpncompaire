## Why Disney+ Has Different Catalogs by Region

Disney+ looks like one service but every region has a distinct license bundle. The US catalog has full Marvel, Pixar, and Star Wars; Disney+ Hotstar (India) adds Bollywood, IPL cricket, and Hindi originals. European Disney+ includes Star Channel adult content; smaller markets have a stripped-down catalog.

Practical breakdown:
- **US catalog**: Full Marvel/Star Wars/Pixar, optional ESPN+ bundle
- **Hotstar India**: Bollywood, IPL, Hindi originals
- **UK Disney+**: Star adult tier, BBC co-productions
- **Smaller markets**: Reduced catalog, some Marvel titles delayed

## BBC iPlayer: Why UK-Only?

BBC iPlayer is funded by the UK TV License fee. Opening it to non-UK users isn't legally or ethically aligned with BBC's funding model — international viewers are served by BBC Studios as a separate commercial product.

iPlayer checks IP geolocation, payment region, and device language. From a non-UK IP you get the "BBC iPlayer only works in the UK" error.

## Hulu: Can You Access It Without a US Card?

Hulu is US-only and requires a US payment method. The Disney+/Hulu bundle also needs a US billing address. VPN solves the IP barrier but not the payment one — you can't sign up for Hulu with VPN alone.

## Three VPN Selection Criteria

### 1. Streaming-Optimized Servers

Premium providers maintain separate IP pools for Disney+, BBC iPlayer, and Hulu. If pools aren't labeled, you'll need to test servers manually — but large-pool providers usually have 1-2 working servers at any time.

### 2. Speed and Stability

iPlayer streams 4K UHD; Disney+ has a 4K Ultra HD plan. Slow VPNs stutter even at 1080p. See our [speed and performance optimization](/en/blog/vpn-speed-optimization) guide.

### 3. DNS and IPv6 Leak Protection

iPlayer in particular catches DNS queries that leak to your real region. If your VPN doesn't route DNS through its own resolvers, you're blocked even with a UK IP. Our [streaming and content access article](/en/blog/vpn-streaming-and-content-access) covers leak testing.

## Step-by-Step Disney+ and iPlayer Access

1. **Pick a streaming-certified VPN**: Premium provider with UK and US coverage
2. **Connect to a server**: US East Coast for Disney+, London for iPlayer
3. **Clear browser cache**: Old IP cookies cause issues

6. **If not working**: Switch to a different city in the same country

## Account Region vs IP Region

Important distinction: Netflix shows the catalog of your IP region — account region doesn't matter. Disney+ is different: it sometimes prefers account region, sometimes IP. Hotstar checks account region exclusively.

So a EU-registered Disney+ account connecting to US VPN often still shows the EU catalog. Disney+ US catalog requires a US-registered account, which means signing up while connected to US VPN with a US payment method.
