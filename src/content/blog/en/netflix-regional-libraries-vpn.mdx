Can't find the show you want on Netflix in your country? Getting "this content isn't available in your region"? Netflix's US, Japan, UK, and Canada libraries are 5-10x larger than smaller-market versions and contain very different content. This guide covers Netflix regional library differences, how to access them with VPN, and which VPNs actually work.

## Why Netflix Has Different Content per Country

Licensing. Netflix buys broadcast rights country by country. If a film isn't licensed for a region, it's not shown there — not Netflix's choice but the rights holder's contract terms.

Result:
- **US library**: ~6,000+ titles, broadest catalog
- **UK**: Slightly smaller, high-quality
- **Japan**: Strong anime selection, lighter on Western content
- **Smaller markets**: ~4,000-4,500 titles, significant gaps

Same subscription, same monthly fee, very different content.

## Is the Netflix VPN Block Real?

Yes. Since 2016 Netflix aggressively detects and blocks VPN traffic. Methods:

- **IP pool analysis**: Hundreds of accounts from one IP = VPN/proxy
- **DNS leak**: If your DNS queries leak outside the VPN, Netflix sees real location
- **Third-party databases**: MaxMind, IP2Location flag VPN IPs

The Netflix VPN block often manifests not as an error but as **showing you the local catalog despite VPN connection** — meaning your VPN is "working" but Netflix is ignoring it.

## Step-by-Step Use

1. **Pick a streaming-certified VPN**: Wide US/UK coverage
2. **Connect to a server**: US East Coast (NY, NJ) is closest to Europe/Turkey
3. **Clear browser cache**: Netflix remembers old IPs
4. **Open Netflix**: Same account, the new library should load automatically
5. **If not working**: Switch servers (different city in the same country) and retry
6. **Still not working**: Wait 24h (IP pool refreshes) or try a different provider

## Will Your Subscription Cost Change?

No. Netflix subscription pricing is tied to your account's registration country, not the VPN region. As a Turkey/EU subscriber connecting to US, your fee doesn't change but your library does.

Exception: If you're creating a new account while connected via VPN, Netflix may register you to that country's pricing — sometimes cheaper. Same logic applies to [ChatGPT Plus](/en/blog/chatgpt-plus-pricing-vpn-savings), [YouTube Premium](/en/blog/youtube-premium-cheap-vpn), and [Spotify](/en/blog/spotify-regional-pricing-vpn).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Netflix forbid VPN use?**
Netflix's VPN, region and account policies can change. Check the current terms and help documentation for possible access denial, catalogue changes or other account actions.

**Why doesn't my VPN work even though it's connected?**
Likely the IP pool is on Netflix's blocklist. Try a different server in the same country.

**Free VPNs for Netflix?**
Almost all are blocked. Premium streaming requires premium VPN — comparison in our [free vs paid article](/en/blog/free-vs-paid-vpn).

**4K streaming with VPN possible?**
Yes, with a quality provider over WireGuard. Throughput must consistently exceed 25 Mbps.
