AI agents can now browse websites, use connected applications and complete multi-step tasks. That creates a privacy model that is broader than ordinary browser traffic.

## Where a VPN helps

A VPN can protect the network path between your local device and the internet, especially on untrusted Wi-Fi. It can also change the IP address seen by services you access directly from that device.

## Where it may not help

A remote agent may run inside a cloud-hosted browser. Traffic from that remote browser may not pass through the VPN running on your laptop or phone.

## The bigger risks

Agent permissions, connected accounts, session cookies and prompt injection often matter more than the local IP address. An agent with access to email or cloud storage can reach sensitive data even when the network tunnel is perfectly secure.

## 2026 checklist

- Connect only the apps the agent needs.
- Use passkeys or MFA on connected accounts.
- Review active sessions.
- Avoid broad instructions for sensitive financial or administrative tasks.
- Require confirmation for destructive actions.
- Clear remote browser sessions when appropriate.

## Bottom line

A VPN remains a valuable network layer, but AI-agent security is primarily about permissions, account boundaries and supervised actions. Treat those controls separately.
