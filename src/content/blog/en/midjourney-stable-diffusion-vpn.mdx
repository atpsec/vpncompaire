## Midjourney and Discord Access

Midjourney started as a Discord bot and still relies heavily on Discord (a web UI was added in 2024). That creates two access layers:

**Discord availability**: Discord has had intermittent access issues in Turkey (briefly blocked by court order in 2023, then restored). For stable use, a VPN may be required. A WireGuard tunnel works smoothly even in Discord voice channels without noticeable lag.

**Midjourney payment**: Midjourney starts at $10/month (Basic, Standard, Pro, Mega tiers). Turkish credit cards occasionally fail with "payment failed." US or EU IP plus an international card is the most stable route.

**Web interface**: midjourney.com lets you manage generations and account. Access here is less problematic than Discord.

## Stable Diffusion: Local and Online

Stable Diffusion is open source and runs in two modes:

**Local install**: AUTOMATIC1111, ComfyUI, Forge run on Windows/Mac/Linux. Here VPN need is minimal — only model downloads from Hugging Face may require VPN for some restricted models.

**Online services**: Replicate, RunDiffusion, Stable Diffusion Online, Leonardo.ai, Civitai, Tensor.art host Stable Diffusion in the cloud. Most work from Turkey, but VPN helps with payment and regional pricing.

The most common Turkey-side block is access to NSFW or restricted models in repositories. A US or EU VPN bypasses this. For bandwidth performance, see our [VPN speed optimization guide](/en/blog/vpn-speed-optimization).

## Privacy During Image Generation

Reference images, prompts, and generation history get stored on provider servers. For sensitive content (personal photos, copyrighted references, internal designs), use two layers:

1. **Keep VPN active**: Hides IP and location metadata. The provider can't track you geographically.
2. **Account settings**: Use Midjourney "stealth mode" (Pro+ plans), DALL-E "do not use my data for training," and similar provider-specific opt-outs.

Our [VPN streaming and content access article](/en/blog/vpn-streaming-and-content-access) covers regional content limits broadly. AI content policy and censorship are different dimensions, but the geo layer is the same.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I run Midjourney from Turkey?**
midjourney.com web UI works from Turkey. For the Discord bot, you need Discord accessible — a VPN helps against intermittent blocks. Use a US or EU IP for payment for stability.

**Do I need a VPN for Stable Diffusion?**
Local installs don't need one. Online services (Replicate, Leonardo) generally load from Turkey, but VPN helps with payment and regional pricing.

**Can my IP leak during AI generation?**
Yes, particularly with local Stable Diffusion installs where some plugins (Civitai integration, model fetch) hit the internet directly. VPN masks this traffic. Choose a VPN with DNS leak protection.
