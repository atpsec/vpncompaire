Before buying, "is there a trial, can I get a refund?" are fair questions. Money-back promises differ by provider — fine print matters. This guide compares trial types and refund flows.

## Trial Types

| Type | What you get | Risk |
|------|--------------|------|
| **Free tier** | Limited data/speed | Data sales, ads — [free vs paid](/blog/free-vs-paid-vpn) |
| **Money-back guarantee** | 7–30 day full refund | Terms and payment channel |
| **Card trial** | 3–7 days premium | Auto-renewal |
| **Official demo** | Rare, limited | May not be enough to test |

"Trial" sometimes means free tier only — not the same as money-back.

## Reading Money-Back Terms

Checklist:

1. **Window** — 7, 14, 30 or 45 days? Our [provider profiles](/vpn-reviews) link to the official `moneyBackDays` information.
2. **Full vs partial** — first subscription only?
3. **Payment channel** — App Store / Play refund rules differ from direct billing
4. **Crypto** — often no refund
5. **Auto-renewal** — cancel before renewal date

See [cancel & refund](/refund-policy) for provider summaries.

## Monthly vs Yearly and Refunds

Annual plans discount price; refund window may differ. [Monthly vs yearly](/blog/vpn-subscription-monthly-vs-yearly) compares flexibility.

During the window test speed, [kill switch](/blog/vpn-kill-switch-explained) and [DNS protection](/blog/dns-leak-test-vpn).

## Refund Tips

- Keep invoice and subscription date
- Request via support or account panel before deadline
- App Store subs go through Apple/Google
- After refund account closes — read data deletion policy

## Red Flags

- Lifetime one-time deals — no refund
- Vague "satisfaction" wording
- Phone-only refund (sales pressure)
- Full annual prepayment for "trial"
