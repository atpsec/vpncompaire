Obfuscation and multi-hop are frequently presented as “advanced privacy” features, but they solve different problems.

## Obfuscation

Obfuscation changes how VPN traffic appears on the network. The goal is to reduce simple protocol identification or blocking. It can help on restrictive networks, but it may add overhead and is not needed on every connection.

## Multi-hop

Multi-hop routes traffic through two or more VPN servers before it reaches the internet. This changes the trust and routing model, but it also adds latency and can reduce throughput.

## Which problem are you solving?

Use obfuscation when the problem is **VPN detection or blocking**. Consider multi-hop when the problem is **reducing reliance on a single VPN server path** and you accept the performance cost.

## What to compare

- Protocol support
- Server locations
- Speed impact
- Mobile battery use
- Whether the feature is independently documented
- Whether it works with kill switch and split tunnelling

## Bottom line

Do not pay for advanced features just because they sound more secure. Choose the feature that matches a specific threat model and test its real performance on your network.
